Backups

Download Full Backup

A full backup is one zip with the database, the signing keys and every uploaded receipt, document and photo. Keep a copy somewhere other than this server.

Automatic daily backup
Saves a copy of the database on this server about once a day, on the first visit after 24 hours. These are small (no uploads) and protect against a mistake or a bad update, not against losing the server's disk.
Automatic backups to keep
Older automatic backups are deleted as new ones are made. Ones you make by hand are kept until you delete them.
